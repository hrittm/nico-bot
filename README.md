# Intro

Hello!! It's Nico.   
A Discord bot inspired by the brilliant archaeologist from the anime [*One Piece*](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/One_Piece_Wiki), Nico Robin. 

# Character Info

**Nickname**: Light of the Revolution / Demon Child  
**Bounty**: ฿930,000,000  
**Devil Fruit**: [Hana Hana no Mi](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Hana_Hana_no_Mi) (Flower-Flower Fruit)  

**Nico Robin** is an archaeologist and wise character who can read Poneglyphs and researches ancient history. She is incredibly calm, articulate, highly observant, and deeply knowledgeable. She maintains a gentle, warm, and encouraging demeanor, treating complex problems as intriguing mysteries. 

She became a scholar at a very young age. However, due to researching the Void Century, her home island of Ohara was attacked by the [World Government](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/World_Government) via a Buster Call. The island was completely destroyed and all the scholars were killed, including Nico Robin's mother, Nico Olvia. But Nico Robin somehow managed to escape and got a huge bounty of ฿79,000,000 on her head at just eight years old. Because of this, she was known as the 'Demon Child'.  

Later, she traveled to various islands and survived by partnering with various underground groups and pirate crews. She met [Monkey D. Luffy](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Monkey_D._Luffy) and his crew during the Alabasta Arc. After the defeat of [Sir Crocodile](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Crocodile), Luffy rescued her from the collapsing underground temple, and she chose to join the [Straw Hat Pirates](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Straw_Hat_Pirates). Through them, she finally found true friends who were willing to declare war on the world just to protect her. During the two-year time-skip, she spent her time training and researching alongside the [Revolutionary Army](https://onepiece.fandom.com/wiki/Revolutionary_Army).

# Features

Nico is designed to bring her signature calm intellectual depth right into your Discord server, acting as an advanced academic and analytical assistant.

*   **Academic & Logical Excellence**: Nico excels at breaking down complex concepts in Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Mathematics (Pure/Applied, Olympiad-style logic), Computer Science, and Formal Logic.
*   **Step-by-Step Problem Solving**: Instead of just dumping raw answers, she guides you through problems step-by-step so you can truly grasp the underlying mechanisms.
*   **Conversational Memory Buffer**: She retains a contextual memory of the last 20 messages per channel, allowing you to have flowing, natural discussions without repeating your prompts.
*   **Slash Command Integration**: Features native Discord slash commands like `/solve` for target research queries and `/clear` to reset her conversational memory slate.
*   **Elegant Formatting**: Technical responses are meticulously organized using clean Markdown tables, bullet points, and LaTeX formatting for clear math/science equations.
*   **24/7 Monitoring Ready**: Built with a dedicated lightweight HTTP health-check server designed to answer HEAD/GET requests, keeping the bot awake permanently via services like [UptimeRobot](https://uptimerobot.com) and [Render](https://render.com).

# Commands

*   `!prefix` or `@Nico`: Mention Nico or chat in her dedicated channel to start a conversational discussion.
*   `/solve [query]`: Ask Nico an academic question or analytical problem to get a detailed breakdown.
*   `/clear`: Wipes Nico's active memory buffer for the current channel, resetting the conversation.
